Haapsalu urban blocks

A self-study GIS project: Haapsalu (Estonia) cut into urban blocks with BlocksNet and described by land use, population, density, walking accessibility and schools, plus a comparison of OpenStreetMap buildings with the official building data.

Key findings

  • Building-only population estimate is 1.73–2.52× the census (24 % of dwelling floor area vacant is one known reason)
  • Floors: 75.7 % of official buildings vs 3.6 % in OSM
  • 64 OSM buildings carry an ETAK id absent from current official data (possibly demolished, not verified)
  • Shared foot/cycle paths shorten walking time by more than 2 min for 13.5 % of block pairs
  • All children aged 7-15 live within 30 min walk of a municipal basic school; the PGS § 7 (3) 60-minute rule is met (walking assumed)

Population

9,595 people are spread over 186 blocks with dwellings; the 10 most populated blocks hold 27 % of them. The median is 25 people per hectare.

The census total of Haapsalu linn is distributed over the blocks by registry net area of the dwellings; it is not a count per block. The scale is clipped at the 95th percentile (190 people per hectare).

More on population

The census total is distributed over the blocks by the registry net area of the dwellings. For comparison, the same population estimated from floor area would be 16,581 people (1.73 times the census) from the registry net area and 24,189 (2.52 times) from footprint × floors, which is why the total is distributed instead.

Density

Typical built blocks are low and loose: of 216 blocks with buildings the median fsi is 0.31 and the median block has 1.8 floors on average; no block reaches the high-rise group.

Colour the map by fsi, MXI or a BlocksNet morphotype. Hover or clickTap a dot of the chart or a block of the map to find it on both.

More on density

fsi = floor area / block area; gsi = footprint / block area; L = fsi / gsi; OSR = (1 − gsi) / fsi; MXI = living share of the gross floor area. The fsi scale is clipped at the 95th percentile (1.14). Morphotypes are BlocksNet results (thresholds and cluster names are BlocksNet's, not ours).

Accessibility

Walking between blocks takes a median of 19.6 min; the most remote block has a median of 56.0 min to all others.

Walking network of OpenStreetMap at 5 km/h (a conditional assumption). The scale is clipped at the 98th percentile (43 min).

Methods & limitations

Method

  • Blocks. BlocksNet cut_urban_blocks from OSM roads (BlocksNet road types plus highway=service; no footways or paths) and water. Railways are not used. Islands and slivers without buildings are dropped; blocks over 1 km² are marked.
  • Buildings come from the official ETAK + Building Register layer and from OSM; a building belongs to the block that contains its inside point and is not cut.
  • Floors: register value, else height / 3 m (conditional), else 1.
  • Population: census 2021 total distributed by registry net area of dwellings.
  • Land use: cadastral purpose, class with the largest area share.
  • Accessibility: OSM walking network (cycleways without a foot tag are assumed walkable), 5 km/h, shortest of parallel edges; the matrix is stored as float16.
  • Schools: EHIS schools inside the town; demand = children 7-15 distributed by population; BlocksNet competitive_provision with thresholds of 10, 15, 20 minutes.
  • MXI and morphotypes: BlocksNet mxi = living / all floor area (footprint × floors, gross); a building is living if it has a dwelling use (is_dwelling), as a whole. Morphotypes from BlocksNet get_spacematrix_morphotypes and get_strelka_morphotypes for blocks with buildings.
  • Q2: pairing by overlap above 50 % of the smaller building.

Limitations

  • 5 km/h and 3 m per floor are assumptions, not norms.
  • MXI: the building counts as living as a whole, so a shop on the ground floor does not lower it; sheds and garages count as non-living and lower it.
  • The thresholds of the Strelka morphotypes and the names of the Spacematrix clusters come from BlocksNet, not from our norms; the 11 clusters, KMeans, random_state 42 and scikit-learn 1.9.1 are the BlocksNet defaults and the installed version. Different Spacematrix clusters can have the same name.
  • The block point is snapped to the nearest node of the walking network; for a few blocks the choice of the node changes times by minutes. A few blocks are far from the nearest node of the network.
  • Large blocks: the block point can lie in a forest.
  • Land use is legal (cadastre), not actual.
  • Population per block is a distributed total; the census is from 2021 and the pupils from 2026.
  • Places of the schools are the pupils, not the capacity; the age of children by block is unknown.
  • The buildings of the register and OSM are of different dates (OSM was downloaded 2026-10-30, official buildings 2026-10-30).
  • The basemap is informative and unofficial (Maa- ja Ruumiamet); it is not part of the analysis.

The full list with numbers is in PLAN.md in the repository.
