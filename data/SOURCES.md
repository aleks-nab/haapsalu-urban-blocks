## Notes and decisions

- **Living area per person: 29.9 m²** (Haapsalu linn, census 2021) = RL21218 total area of occupied conventional dwellings (286,529 m²) / RL21003 population (9,595). This is the total useful floor area of dwellings, the same concept as the 30.1 m² for Estonia on the census page "Housing arrangements" (https://rahvaloendus.ee/en/results/housing-arrangements). It is the closest to BlocksNet's footprint × floors (gross floor area). The same calculation gives 29.3 m² for Estonia, slightly below 30.1 m², because people in institutions are in the denominator. RL21807 (23.5 m² Estonia, 22.7 m² Haapsalu linn) is not used: it is about 78 % of the total area, which fits the floor area of rooms only (inferred; the table has no footnote).
- **Limitation:** BlocksNet floor area is footprint × floors (gross floor area, with walls, stairs and non-residential floors), while 29.9 m² is a usable floor area per person. The population estimate is therefore expected to be higher than the official 9 595. The parameter is not tuned to the official figure.
- **Railway:** `osm_railways` contains segments of the closed Haapsalu line; they are not used for block cutting.
- **Schools:** only general-education schools inside Haapsalu linn are used (`schools.geojson`). Excluded: Haapsalu Kutsehariduskeskus (vocational), Uuemõisa Lasteaed-Algkool (Uuemõisa alevik) and Ridala Põhikool (Panga küla), both outside the settlement. EHIS gives students, not design capacity; students are used as capacity.
- **OSM schools:** the duplicate outline of Haapsalu Põhikool inside its school grounds is removed.
- **Buildings** (both layers) are kept whole if their inside point lies in the town, not cut at the boundary.
- **Roads:** BlocksNet road types plus `highway=service`; no footway, path, cycleway or steps. The roads layer was downloaded again for this on the date shown in the table (the other OSM layers keep their date).
- **Blocks:** cut with BlocksNet from OSM roads and water, no railway. Islands without buildings (no buildings, further than 20 m from any road, no area limit) are dropped (`blocks_dropped_islands`). Slivers (no buildings, under 500 m²) are dropped (`blocks_dropped_slivers`). Other blocks without buildings are kept with `built_up = False`. Blocks over 1 km² have `large_block = True`. Columns of `blocks.geojson` from the buildings: `population_block` (census population distributed by registry net area of the dwellings), and density indicators from all official buildings: `gsi`, `fsi`, `l`, `osr`, `share_floors_estimated` (floors of all sources).
- **official_buildings.geojson:** the server row number `fid` is dropped and the rows are sorted by `ehr_gid`, then `etak_id`, so the file is identical between runs. Added columns: `footprint_m2` (geometry area), `floors` (registry `max_korruste_arv`, else `round(korgus_m / 3)` with minimum 1, else 1) and `floors_source` (`registry`, `height_3m`, `default_1`) and `is_dwelling` (at least one of five dwelling uses in `kaos_nimetus`; the whole building counts; cut text tails read as the full name for classification only). 3 m per floor is a conventional assumption; floors not from the registry are for density only, not for population.
- **Frozen downloads:** every source is downloaded once; raw files stay in `data/raw/` (not in git) and later runs reuse them. The column *Downloaded* is the date of the first download (`data/download_dates.json`). To refresh a source, delete its raw file and its date entry. The date of the derived `blocks*` layers is the date of the last run.
- **Official buildings, re-download check (2026-10-31):** the WFS layer was requested again one day after the first download. The result was identical: 3 941 buildings in town, 2 982 with `max_korruste_arv`, the same `etak_id` set, no floor value changed, latest `muutmisaeg` 2026-10-22 in both. Only the server's internal row number `fid` (3 903 buildings) and the order of the features differ; geometry and all other attributes are identical. OSM was not downloaded again: it is served from the local copy of the first download (3 885 buildings, 140 with `building:levels`).
